Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

NextMove India (“we”, “our” or “us”) provides online learning programmes and information through nextmoveindia.org. This policy explains how information may be collected and used when you visit our website, contact us, request a demo or participate in our online programmes.

1. Information you provide

When a parent or guardian submits an enquiry or registration form, we may collect information such as the parent's name and contact number, the child's name and age, city, learning interests and details needed to arrange a demo or communicate about a programme. Please do not submit information that is not needed for the enquiry.

2. How we use information

  • Respond to enquiries and communicate with parents or guardians.
  • Arrange demo sessions, classes, enrolment and learner support.
  • Maintain enquiry and class-administration records.
  • Understand website usage, improve pages and measure the effectiveness of our communications.
  • Protect the website, users and our services.

3. Forms and service providers

Website enquiries may be processed using Google Forms/Sheets or Google Apps Script services and may be followed up through WhatsApp, phone or email. Online sessions may use Google Meet or another platform communicated to parents. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control their independent services.

4. Analytics, cookies and advertising

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate website activity. Analytics technologies may use cookies or similar identifiers. If Google AdSense advertising is enabled on this website, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalise ads as permitted by applicable settings and law. Visitors can manage Google's advertising personalisation and browser cookie settings through Google's and their browser's controls. For details, see Google's advertising technologies information and Google's Privacy Policy.

4A. Third-party advertising technologies and information

When advertisements are served on this website, Google and other advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, browser or device identifiers, and information about the page being visited to deliver, measure, limit repetition of, and protect the integrity of advertising. Third parties may place and read cookies in a visitor's browser, or use web beacons and IP addresses, in connection with ads shown on this website. The information handled by those providers is also subject to their own privacy policies and settings. Read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services for more detail.

Visitors can review or manage Google advertising settings at Google Ads Settings. Browser controls may also be used to manage cookies. Disabling cookies may affect some website or advertising functions.

5. Children and parental involvement

Our programmes are intended for children, but website enquiries and enrolment information should be submitted by a parent or legal guardian. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of the website and online services. Please contact us if you believe a child has submitted personal information without appropriate parental involvement.

Because some of our learning programmes are designed for children, advertising and analytics on child-directed pages must be handled in accordance with applicable Google publisher requirements and relevant law. Google does not permit personalised advertising based on past or current activity of users known to be under 13, or on pages directed to children under 13. We do not ask children to provide information for advertising profiles. Parents and guardians should contact us with any concern about data collected through the website.

6. Sharing and retention

We may share relevant information with service providers who help us operate enquiry, communication, analytics and online-class services, or where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights and safety. We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, operational recordkeeping and applicable legal requirements, then delete or de-identify it where appropriate.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the nature of the information. No website, messaging channel or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and requests

A parent or guardian may ask us to access, correct or delete enquiry information, or raise a privacy concern, by emailing info@nextmoveindia.org. We may need to verify the request and retain limited records where required for legitimate operational or legal purposes.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website, services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its revision date.
