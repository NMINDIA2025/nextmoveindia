Parent FAQs

Questions parents often ask

Is this suitable for shy children?

Yes. Activities build gradually from short responses to stories and mini-presentations, with encouragement rather than pressure.

What will my child practise?

Conversation, vocabulary, storytelling, role-play and short presentations, adapted to the learner’s level.

How often are classes held?

Three live classes weekly: two learning sessions and one practice session.

Can we try a class first?

Parents can request a free demo. The team will confirm current availability and suitable batch timing.
