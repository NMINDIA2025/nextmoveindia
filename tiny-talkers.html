Parent FAQs

Questions parents often ask

Does my child need to know English already?

No. The programme is designed as an early, supportive introduction. The teacher can understand your child’s starting point during the demo.

How are preschoolers kept engaged online?

Lessons use songs, visuals, stories, sound games and short speaking turns suited to young learners.

How often are classes held?

Three live classes weekly: two learning sessions and one practice session.

How do I enrol?

Request a free demo through the website form or message the team on WhatsApp. Current batch timing and availability will be confirmed by the team.
