Parent FAQs

Questions parents ask

Which classes does the Online School serve?

The programme is positioned for Pre-School to Class 8. Ask the team to confirm the current availability for your child’s specific class.

Is this the same as the speaking courses?

No. Online School is a separate academic programme. Spoken English and public-speaking courses are skill-building classes that can complement regular schooling.

How many classes are there each week?

The schedule depends on class level and the current school timetable. The team will confirm the weekly routine and subjects before enrolment.

What is included in each plan?

Ask the team to confirm the current inclusions, teacher support, schedule and duration for available school plans before making a decision.

How can I speak with the team?

Use the website enquiry form or message NextMove India on WhatsApp to request school information and a conversation about fit.
