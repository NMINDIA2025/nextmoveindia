How learning works

School partnership and academic records

Teaching by NMI, academic documentation through partner schools

Our teaching role

Next Move India staff conduct the students’ online classes and provide day-to-day teaching support.

Partner-school details

The academic arrangement involves partner schools. The applicable school name and enrolment details should be confirmed with our team for each student before admission.

Marksheets and results

As described in the programme arrangement, student marksheets display the partner school’s details alongside the Next Move India name. Parents should confirm the issuing school, assessment process and recognition applicable to their child’s record before enrolment.

Next Move India provides online teaching. It does not independently claim to be a recognised school or examining board. School enrolment, examinations, marksheets and any formal academic certification are subject to the specific partner-school arrangement.
